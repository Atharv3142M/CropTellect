About Our Project

CropTellect is an AI-powered plant disease detection system designed to help farmers and agricultural professionals identify crop diseases quickly and accurately. Built with TensorFlow, Flask, and a fine-tuned VGG19 model, it supports farmers with reliable diagnostics directly from leaf images.

Our mission is to empower farmers with cutting-edge technology that enables early disease detection, reduces crop losses, and promotes sustainable agricultural practices.

How It Works

1

Upload Image
